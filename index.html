    Methodology Snapshot

    Transparent factors power trustworthy numbers.

    Transport

    Mode-specific emission factors sourced from IPCC, UK BEIS, India GHG Program and Indian Railways (2015–2024). Values are applied per kilometre (kg CO₂ per km) for each transport mode to estimate monthly travel impacts from user-entered distances.

    Diet

    Diet emission factors follow Rodríguez‑Martín et al., Frontiers in Nutrition (2025). Factors are reported as kg CO₂e per person per day and are converted to a monthly estimate in-app by multiplying the daily factor by 30 (monthlyDietCO2 = dailyFactor × 30). These are population-average figures and should be interpreted as standardized estimates, not individual measurements.

    Energy

    Electricity intensity uses the India grid average of 0.727 kg CO₂ per kWh (Central Electricity Authority, CEA India, 2024). This national-average factor is applied to user-entered monthly kWh to produce a monthly electricity emissions estimate; regional or supplier-specific factors can be substituted if available.
